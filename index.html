About LDA

Shaping Lahore into a World-Class City

The Lahore Development Authority was established in 1975 under the Lahore Development Authority Act to plan, develop and regulate the growth of Lahore. Its mandate covers metropolitan planning, housing and estate management, infrastructure development and the enforcement of building and zoning regulations.

The Authority works alongside its two agencies, the Water and Sanitation Agency (WASA) and the Traffic Engineering and Transport Planning Agency (TEPA), to deliver integrated urban services across the Lahore Division.
